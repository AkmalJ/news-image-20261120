One image that sums up the world's news of **Friday 20 November 2026**:
a broadsheet front page with 35 stories — 3 features, 10 pictured cards and
22 briefs — every one of them an event that happened, or was first reported,
on that Friday.

The page leads with the day's three biggest stories: Russian missiles hit
three Kyiv bridges in an overnight barrage of about 110 missiles and 400
drones; Cyclone Akvan came ashore near Gopalpur in Odisha after more than
300,000 people were moved to shelters; and an AI sell-off sank Asia's stock
markets (Taipei −6.2%, Seoul −5.2%, Tokyo −5.1%). Around them: the UK–EU
reset deal, COP31's deal-less final day, a Russian cruise missile in a Polish
field, Japan's 2.3% inflation, Kenya's first oil well, Bukele's third-term
filing, Glenn Phillips' 118 against India, and 22 further briefs, each with
its place and, where it fits, its source. A red DATE CHECK box names the
famous Thursday 19 November stories deliberately left out — the Shenzhen
summit, the World Court's Myanmar ruling, Anthropic's IPO debut, GTA VI's
launch — and the things that had not happened yet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024×1024, about 230 KB) to `/output/entry.png`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`). No network, no dependencies beyond the Python
standard library. `generate.py` is the Pillow program that drew the page;
running it reproduces `entry.png` byte for byte.
